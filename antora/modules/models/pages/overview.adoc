= Choose a built-in model

Import models from `ontolutils.ex`. These modules implement selected terms,
not every class and constraint of their respective ontologies.

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Module | Examples | Use
| `prov` | `Person`, `Organization`, `Entity`, `Activity` | Provenance
| `schema` | `Project`, `ResearchProject`, `SoftwareSourceCode` | Projects and software
| `dcat` | `Dataset`, `Distribution`, `Resource` | Data catalogs
| `m4i` | `NumericalVariable`, `TextVariable`, `Tool` | Scientific variables; needs NumPy
| `sosa` / `ssn` | `Sensor`, `Observation`, `ObservableProperty` | Sensor metadata
| `hdf5` | `File`, `Group`, `Dataset` | HDF5 structure descriptions
| `skos`, `spdx`, `qudt` | `Concept`, `Checksum`, `Unit` | Concepts, checksums, units
|===

Inspect `Model.model_fields` for its declared fields and defaults. Use
`Model.iri("fieldName")` to check a predicate. Models inherit from `Thing` and
support the xref:core:serialization.adoc[same graph serialization methods].
